# 📝 Day 19 Assessment: Gaps, Islands & Financial Streaks

## 🎯 Benchmark Challenges
1. **The Consecutive Login Streak Challenge**: Calculate user retention streaks and identify longest vs current active streak.
2. **The High-Frequency Inactivity Sessionizer**: Group 50M clickstream events into sessions with a 15-minute timeout window.
3. **The Margin Deficit Island**: Detect contiguous trading periods where portfolio maintenance margin fell below regulatory limits.
4. **The Missing Sequence Auditor**: Identify all skipped serial invoice numbers across multi-tenant ledger partitions.
5. **The As-Of Price Interpolator**: Use window boundary frames to forward-fill missing weekend price observations with Friday close prices.
